Data & method

Session analysis, logistic regression, and XGBoost examine booking behavior. Model evaluation uses an 80/20 train-test split. SHAP rankings use mean absolute contributions across the test set; they describe model behavior, not causal effects.

Monthly cohorts follow users from their first observed transaction. The transaction window runs from June 2024 to March 20, 2025; the final month is incomplete.
